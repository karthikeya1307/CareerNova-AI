# Tailored resumes and cover letters

## Creating a cover letter
Open the Documents page, choose Cover letter, pick the resume to use and select the internship. You can also start from an internship's page with "Write a cover letter". Choose a tone (formal or concise) and, optionally, up to 10 skills from your resume to emphasise. Then generate the letter.

## What the cover letter uses
The letter is built for the selected internship. It uses the internship's title, company, domain, required and preferred skills, responsibilities and education requirement, together with facts from your parsed resume: your skills, degree, projects and experience. Your most relevant projects and experience for that internship are chosen first.

## No invented facts
The letter only uses information from your resume. It never claims a skill you do not have and never invents experience, employers or numbers. The posting's responsibilities are quoted only as the posting's words, and only when they name skills you have. It mentions that you are learning a missing skill only if you marked that skill as in progress in your skill-gap plan. A fact check lists anything that needs your review.

## Tailored resumes
A tailored resume reorders and selects the skills, projects and experience from your resume that are most relevant to the internship. You can set how many skills, projects, experience entries and bullet points to include. An ATS check shows which of the internship's keywords your resume already covers.

## Editing, versions and downloads
You can edit the wording of a generated document; each save creates a new version that is checked again. You can restore earlier versions and download the document as DOCX or plain text.

## Optional AI-assisted writing
AI-assisted writing is off by default. If the server has an AI provider configured, you can turn it on for a document after reading the disclosure and giving consent. Every AI-written sentence is checked against your resume, and unsupported sentences are removed.
