# Applications and the career assistant

## Tracking applications
Mark an internship as applied from its page, or add an application you made elsewhere on the Applications page. Statuses are Saved, Applied, Shortlisted, Interview scheduled, Selected, Rejected and Withdrawn, and only allowed status changes are offered. You can undo the last change, see the full history, add company interview rounds and keep notes.

## Reminders
You can add reminders to an application. Reminders are stored in the application and shown on the Applications page and dashboard; no email, SMS or push notification is sent.

## Saved internships and trash
Saved internships appear on the Applications page, where you can open them, mark them as applied or remove them. Applications you delete go to the trash first, where you can restore them or delete them permanently.

## The career assistant
The career assistant answers questions about your own data, such as your skills, best matches, skill gaps, documents, interview practice and applications, and questions about how CareerNova AI works. Answers about your data show the sources they are based on, and answers about the product are based on this product guide. The assistant only reads your data; it never changes anything. It can suggest next steps that open the right page.

## Conversations and follow-up questions
Conversations are saved so you can reopen them later, rename them or delete them. The assistant remembers the recent questions in a conversation, so you can ask follow-up questions such as "and which file types are supported?" after asking about resume upload.

## What the assistant cannot answer
The assistant only answers questions about CareerNova AI and your career data in it. For unrelated topics, or product details that are not in this guide, it says so instead of guessing.
