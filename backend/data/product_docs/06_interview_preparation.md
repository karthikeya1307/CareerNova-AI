# Interview preparation

## Starting a practice session
Open the Interview practice page, choose your resume and, optionally, the internship you are preparing for. Choose practice or mock mode, a difficulty (mixed, easy, medium or hard) and how many technical, project, behavioural and internship-specific questions you want, from 1 to 15 in total.

## Practice and mock modes
In practice mode the model answer and key points are visible while you answer. In mock mode they stay hidden until you answer or reveal them, and you can set an optional time limit of 30 to 900 seconds per question.

## Where the questions come from
Technical questions come from a curated question bank for the skills on your resume and in the internship. Project questions are based on your own projects and experience. Behavioural questions come from a bank of common interview questions, and internship-specific questions are based on the selected internship.

## How answers are evaluated
Each answer is scored from 0 to 100 with a rubric: key points covered and missed, a STAR structure check for behavioural answers, length and clarity. You get feedback, tips and the model answer. Technical scores measure key-point coverage; they do not verify that every statement is technically correct. You can try a question again as many times as you like before finishing the session.

## Session results
When you finish a session you see the overall score (unanswered questions count as zero), scores by category, strengths, areas to improve and related skills from your learning plan. Practice sessions are separate from real company interviews, which you track on the Applications page.
