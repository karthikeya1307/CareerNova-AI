# Recommended workflow

## Getting started: what to do first
Create an account with your email address and verify it using the link sent by email. Then sign in. You can choose "Remember me" to stay signed in on that browser.

## Step-by-step workflow
1. Create your account, verify your email and sign in.
2. Complete your profile on the Profile page: college, degree, graduation year, location and a short bio. Your skills are read from your resume, not typed into the profile.
3. Upload your resume (PDF or DOCX, up to 5 MB) on the Resumes page. CareerNova reads your contact details, skills, education, experience, internships and projects.
4. Check the extracted details on the Resumes page and use "Edit details" to correct anything that was read wrongly. Matching, documents and the assistants use your corrected version.
5. Review the resume readiness score on the Resumes page. The ATS check on the dashboard compares your resume with your best internship match.
6. Run matching on the Matches page to rank internships against your resume, or browse and search the Internships page.
7. Open Skill gaps to see which skills a matched internship needs that your resume does not show yet, with a learning plan.
8. Open the Interview Preparation Assistant to get roles that fit your resume and prepare for interviews.
9. Practise technical and HR questions and mock interviews on the Interview practice page, and follow your preparation roadmap.
10. Keep your profile and resume up to date: upload a new resume version when you improve it and mark your preferred version.

## The dashboard
The dashboard shows your latest resume, your best internship match, an ATS check, your skills and gaps, interview practice progress, your applications and recent assistant conversations. Each card links to the page where you can do more.

## Applying and tracking
Create a tailored resume and a cover letter for an internship on the Documents page. Mark the internship as applied and track its status, interview rounds and reminders on the Applications page.
